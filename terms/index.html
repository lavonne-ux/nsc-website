Consultations, cancellations, and rescheduling

Consultation fees are paid at booking and are non-refundable.

  • A second late change, or a missed session without notice, forfeits the consultation fee. A new session then requires the full consultation fee.

To reschedule, use the link in your confirmation email or contact services@norrilssignature.com.
