Systems architecture and platform builds

Systems your business runs on, built around how it actually works.

Once the records are in order, NSC architects the system that keeps the work moving, then builds the platform that runs it: a client portal, an intake and payment flow, or an internal tool that puts your process into software. You own every account it runs on.

How an engagement works

Every engagement starts with the Discovery Assessment, which maps how the business actually operates. NSC then architects the system, quotes the build in writing, builds the platform, and hands it over with documentation.

You own it

The client owns the hosting, database, and payment accounts the platform runs on, and pays those providers directly. NSC builds and documents the system inside your accounts, so nothing is held hostage.

Support after launch

The Platform Support Plan covers maintenance, monitoring, and fixes for $250 a month. New features are quoted separately in writing.
