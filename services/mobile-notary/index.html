Areas served

Atlanta, South Fulton, College Park, East Point, Union City, Fairburn, Hapeville, and the surrounding communities of Fulton and Clayton Counties, including the Hartsfield-Jackson airport area. Ask about locations farther out.

Before your appointment

Each signer needs a current government-issued photo ID. Leave the documents unsigned until the notary is present, and have every signer at the appointment.

Fees

The notarial fee is $2.00 per act, the amount set by Georgia law. Travel within metro Atlanta is $50.00, or $62.50 with the Administrative Fee, and is confirmed when you book.

Questions

Common questions

Can a notary tell me what my document means?

No. A notary verifies identity and witnesses signatures but cannot explain or prepare legal documents for you.

Do you offer remote online notarization?

Not at this time. Appointments are in person.
