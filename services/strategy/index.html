Assessment, strategy, and funding readiness

Know exactly where your company stands before you file anything.

Every NSC engagement starts with the facts: what is on record with the state, what your documents say, and what is missing. From there you get a sequenced plan, and when you are ready to borrow, a lender-ready package.

Book a ConsultationPay Online

Start with a Strategy Consultation

A 60-minute paid consultation covers where the business stands, what it needs, and the order to do it in. The consultation is a flat $250, booked and paid online, which includes the Administrative Fee and card processing. The fee is credited toward any engagement that begins within 30 days.

Discovery Assessment

NSC verifies each entity against state records, reviews your governing documents and compliance history, and reports every gap in writing with an itemized cost proposal. It covers up to three entities, and additional entities are $100 each.

Implementation Roadmap and structure charts

The Roadmap turns the findings into phases, dependencies, timing, and cost, so you fund the work in the right order. Master Structure Charts show who owns and controls each company in a multi-entity structure, which is often the first document a lender or certifying agency asks to see.

Business plans and funding readiness

The Comprehensive Business Plan includes an executive summary, market analysis, operations, financial projections, and two revision rounds. The Funding Readiness Package assembles the borrowing resolution, incumbency certificate, collateral schedule, ownership chart, and debt schedule a lender reviews. Property moving into a corporation is coordinated with a licensed attorney, who prepares the deed.

Questions

Common questions

Is the consultation fee credited?

Yes. The Strategy Consultation fee is credited toward an engagement that begins within 30 days. Booking and payment happen together on the online booking page, and consultation fees are non-refundable. See the Terms of Use for rescheduling.

Do you work with companies outside Georgia?

Yes. NSC works online with clients across the United States and abroad, for companies formed in any U.S. state.

Is NSC a lender?

No. NSC prepares the records and package a lender reviews. NSC is not a lender, a law firm, or a CPA firm.
