SBA WOSB and EDWOSB

NSC prepares and submits your application through the SBA certification portal, with the ownership and control documentation the program requires.

WBENC: two levels of service

Application Management is for businesses whose corporate records are already complete and current, and includes three follow-ups. Full-Service Certification adds the record rebuild the application usually requires: bylaws, consents, share records, an ownership history narrative, the owner resume, and site visit preparation. Annual recertification is available for certified businesses.

Questions

Common questions

Which WBENC option do I need?

If your bylaws, consents, and share records are complete and current, Application Management fits. If they are missing or inconsistent, Full-Service Certification includes the rebuild. The Discovery Assessment will tell you which.

Is the certification fee included?

No. The certifying organization's fee is separate and paid at cost.
