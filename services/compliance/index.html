State compliance and registrations

Keep every entity active and in good standing.

A missed annual report can dissolve a company without notice. NSC files, tracks, and repairs state compliance for entities in any U.S. state, and reinstates companies that have already lapsed.

Book a ConsultationStart Your Intake

Annual reports and the Annual Compliance Plan

NSC prepares and files annual reports and registrations in any state. The Annual Compliance Plan adds the annual written consent and a compliance calendar, so nothing comes due unnoticed.

Reinstatement

If the state has administratively dissolved your entity, NSC files the reinstatement with any catch-up annual reports and confirms the company is back in good standing.

Expanding to new states

Foreign qualification registers your company to do business in another state, including the home-state good standing certificate the filing requires. NSC also handles amendments, securities exemption notices, business licenses, DBA filings, and registered agent setup.

Questions

Common questions

My company was dissolved. Can it be saved?

In most states, yes. Reinstatement restores the entity once the missing reports and fees are filed. NSC confirms the requirements for your state in the Discovery Assessment.

Do you charge for a D-U-N-S number?

No. D-U-N-S setup is free. The business completes Dun & Bradstreet's verification call.

Are state fees included?

No. State, county, and federal fees are passed through at cost and listed separately on every quote.
