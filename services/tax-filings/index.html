Tax filings

Corporate tax filings, prepared in house.

NSC prepares federal corporate returns and the IRS elections and applications a corporation needs: S corporation elections, 501(c)(3) status, and responsible party or address changes.

Book a ConsultationPay Online

Corporate returns

Form 1120 for a corporation with no activity is a flat $300 per return. Returns with activity are prepared from client-provided books and quoted from $750.

Elections and applications

Form 2553 includes the eligibility review, effective date analysis, filing, and follow-up until the IRS accepts the election. Form 1023-EZ covers 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status; the IRS user fee is separate.

Questions

Common questions

Do you prepare individual returns?

NSC focuses on corporate returns and elections. Ask during your consultation if you need a referral.

Can a corporation with no activity skip filing?

Generally no. A corporation files Form 1120 each year even with no income or expenses.
