Flat, written fees

Every engagement is quoted in writing before work begins, and a quoted fee does not change once the engagement starts.

Accuracy is the product

Fees reflect the time it takes to verify every record, cite every rule correctly, and deliver documents that stand up to a lender, a bank, a state agency, or an attorney's review.

Custom documents start at $300

Every governing document, resolution, and agreement is drafted for the client's state, ownership, and structure.

Administrative Fee

Each invoice carries the Administrative Fee in Section 8. The "With Admin Fee" column shows a service billed on its own invoice. Combined services cost less because the fee caps at $250.

Attorney review

Services marked "Yes" are reviewed by NSC's attorney before delivery. Review is billed at the attorney's rate plus 10% and listed as its own line on every quote.

Government fees at cost

State, county, and federal fees, registered agent service, and other third-party costs are passed through at cost and listed separately.

The Administrative Fee is 15% of professional fees, with a $25 minimum and a $250 maximum, and never more than 25% of professional fees. It does not apply to government fees, the notarial fee set by Georgia law, attorney review, other pass-through costs, or payment processing fees. It is due with each invoice, collected before work begins, and non-refundable once work begins.

10. Payment Terms

  • Zelle to lavonne@norrilssignature.com is preferred and carries no processing fee.
  • All quotes are issued in writing. New scope is quoted separately in writing before it begins.
  • Payment is due with each invoice, and no work begins before payment.
  • Fees for delivered work are non-refundable. Fees for work not yet started are credited toward future NSC services for twelve (12) months, then expire. No cash refunds are issued.
